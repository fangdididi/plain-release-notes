# What's new in Jotter 1.5

Search reaches inside your attachments, and you can pin the notes you use most.

## Before you upgrade

- **Run your own sync server?** Upgrade it to 2.0 or newer first. Older servers refuse pinned notes and stop syncing. <!-- src: #44 -->

## When you search

- **Search looks inside attachments.** Text in PDFs and images now turns up in results. <!-- src: #41 -->
- **Results come back faster:** about 0.4 s on 10000 notes, down from 2.5 s. <!-- src: #41 -->

## When you organise notes

- **Pin a note to keep it at the top** of every list. <!-- src: #44 -->

## When you share or export

- **Markdown exports keep your line breaks.** <!-- src: #43 -->

## Settings

- **Dark mode can follow your system setting.** <!-- src: #47 -->
